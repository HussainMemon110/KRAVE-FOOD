How We Do It

THE CRAFT

01
Source Obsessively
Every ingredient is chosen for flavor first. We visit farms, markets, and suppliers in person — no shortcuts.
02
Prep With Precision
Our brines, marinades, and ferments are timed to the hour. Flavor is built in layers, not added at the end.
03
Cook to Order

Our Story

BORN IN
THE STREETS

KRAVE started as a single food truck in 2019 — a 10-item menu, a line around the block every lunch. We grew up on street food from Tokyo, Mexico City, Seoul, and Istanbul. We built a kitchen around everything we couldn't stop thinking about.
